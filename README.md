# Supermariorpg Browser Game Guide
## What is it?

Supermariorpg is a browser-based action role-playing game where you create your character, explore an open-world environment, engage in combat with monsters, collect resources, and level up to increase your abilities.


Gameplay Overview:

• Create your character from scratch or choose from pre-made ones
• Explore a vast world divided into different zones, each with unique terrain, enemies, and rewards
• Engage in turn-based battles against monstrous creatures
• Collect resources such as food, materials, and magical items to upgrade your equipment and abilities
• Level up to increase your character's stats and unlock new skills

## How to Play (Controls and Objective)


Controls:

• Use the mouse or touch controls to move your character around the world
• Use the left-click button to attack enemies
• Use the right-click button to equip equipment, use items, or interact with objects
• Use the F1-F12 keys to switch between different game modes (e.g. combat, exploration)
• Use the spacebar to toggle map mode


Objective:

• Complete quests and missions to earn experience points and level up
• Explore the world to find hidden items, resources, and secrets
• Defeat powerful monsters to increase your reputation and gain access to new areas
• Master your character's abilities and equipment to become a top player
